WHAT WAS CHANGED

The engine no longer plays chess. It plays "Intransitive" (the rps-v6 rules): a
9x9 board game with rock, paper and scissors pieces in a non-transitive capture
relation, won by reaching the opponent's goal square. The change is pervasive --
it replaces the rules of the game, not a heuristic. Modified or added files:

  types.h          piece types are ROCK/PAPER/SCISSORS; the prey relation
  position.h/.cpp  board state, goal squares, capture legality, FEN
  movegen.cpp      move generation, including the evasion rule
  movepick.cpp     move ordering for the new move set
  bitboard.h       attack/adjacency tables for the new geometry
  evaluate.cpp     a 4-line handcrafted evaluation replaces NNUE
  search.cpp       terminal detection and the 200-ply no-capture draw
  thread.cpp       root move handling
  engine.cpp       UCI identification and option set
  perft.h          perft over the new move generator

Everything else is upstream Stockfish 19, unmodified.
